# KaTuro App — versions

Numbering: **vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH**. Fixes raise the last number (v1.0.1), new features the
middle one (v1.1.0), big changes the first (v2.0.0). The notes under each version are what
teachers see in the app when the update is offered.

## v1.5.0
A fresh look, the chat first, and the camera one tap away.
- A new welcome animation after you sign in.
- The app opens on the chat with your assistant. Your profile photo is at the top and beside your messages.
- Camera button at the top and beside the chat box: check bubble sheets, or take photos of test papers, lesson materials, documents or activities.
- Photos go to KaTuroDesk into Lessons › Phone Photos, sorted by kind. Away from the Wi-Fi they wait on the phone and go by themselves when you are back.
- The KaTuro logo is a little smaller on the sign-in screen and at the bottom.

## v1.4.0
Class alerts and faster bubble sheet checking.
- Class alerts: your phone rings before each class in your KaTuroDesk schedule (15 minutes before, or choose 5, 10, 30, 60 or Off on the Today tab).
- Scan sheets: hold the phone over each bubble sheet and it reads them one after another by itself, with a beep and the learner's score. No shutter button.
- Scan the answer key: shade one sheet as the key and scan it instead of typing.
- The scores still go to your E-Class Record in Records after your review.

## v1.3.0
Linking with your computer is quick.
- Point the camera at the code and it links at once: a smaller code, a sharper camera, the fastest way to your computer.
- Works even when the phone's clock is off.
- Fixed: after choosing a PIN the app stayed on "Once more".
- Needs KaTuroDesk 1.20.0.

## v1.2.0
You choose what your phone sees, on KaTuroDesk (Settings → Phones).
- Each folder: hidden, view, view & add, or full.
- Other folders from your computer can be added for the phone.
- Full folders: rename, move or replace files from the phone (the old file is kept in KaTuro Backups).
- Needs KaTuroDesk 1.18.0.

## v1.1.0
A new, quieter look.
- Soft, clean screens with less text.
- The KaTuro logo in the middle of the bottom bar opens the Assistant.
- A simpler sign-in and PIN screen.

## v1.0.0
The first KaTuro App, linked to KaTuroDesk on your computer.
- Today: your classes, your calendar and the DepEd school calendar; reminders ring on the phone.
- Scores: type scores, check bubble sheets with the camera, or snap checked papers; attendance by tap; undo.
- Assistant: Matt, Luna, Grey and Lola Carmen, working on KaTuroDesk for you.
- Messages, and your Records and Lessons in Files: open, send photos and files, scan to PDF, keep on the phone.
- Private: your class records stay on your computer and this phone only, protected by your PIN.
