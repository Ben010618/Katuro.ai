# Routines pilot: 10–20 teachers, 2 weeks

The last phase of the Routine Builder plan. KaTuroDesk 1.26.0 and the KaTuro App 1.9.0 have everything the pilot needs; this page is how to run it.

## Who
10–20 teachers in one or two schools, mixed on purpose:
- about half who are comfortable with computers, half who are not;
- at least 3 class advisers (for the SF9 routine) and at least 3 who use the KaTuro App;
- one ICT coordinator or master teacher who can help the others.

## Day 1 (30 minutes, in person)
1. Update KaTuroDesk to 1.26.0 (it updates by itself) and the KaTuro App to 1.9.0.
2. Open **Routines** (top bar) → **Ready-made**.
3. Each teacher switches on **one** routine they need this week (most will pick *Friday attendance wrap-up*):
   **Use this** → choose the red cards' files → **Save** → close → switch it **on** in My routines.
4. Show **Try** (it lights the cards and changes nothing) and **Try aloud**.
5. Show **Waiting for your OK** on the KaTuroDesk and on the phone's Today tab.
6. Let one teacher build one by typing a sentence ("Tuwing Biyernes, i-check ang SF2 ko…").

## During the 2 weeks
- Teachers use KaTuroDesk as usual; routines run while it is open.
- The helper checks in once in week 1 (chat or in person): anything stuck in red?

## Day 14: what to collect
1. Each teacher opens **Routines → History → How it is going → Copy** and sends the text. It has counts only: routines, runs, approvals, a rough time estimate. No learner names and no file names.
2. A 5-question survey (English / Filipino):
   1. Which routine did you use most? / Aling routine ang pinakaginamit mo?
   2. How much time did it save you in a normal week (minutes)? / Gaano karaming oras ang natipid mo sa karaniwang linggo (minuto)?
   3. Was anything hard to understand? / May bahagi bang mahirap intindihin?
   4. Did "Ask me first" ever stop a mistake? / Napigilan ba ng "Itanong muna sa akin" ang isang mali?
   5. Would you keep using it? (yes / maybe / no) / Gagamitin mo pa ba ito? (oo / siguro / hindi)

## Success (from the feasibility study)
| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Teachers who switched on a routine in week 1 | at least half |
| Teachers with a routine still on after 2 weeks ("on for 2+ weeks") | at least a third |
| Time saved (survey, and the rough estimate) | reported weekly hours saved |
| Records changed without approval | zero (the app makes this impossible; check it stayed so) |

## Short video script (1 minute, Filipino and English)
1. *"Ito ang Routines. Mga card na gumagawa ng paulit-ulit mong trabaho."* / "These are Routines: cards that do your repeated work."
2. Show Ready-made → Friday attendance wrap-up → Use this. *"Piliin ang SF2 mo, i-save, i-on."* / "Choose your SF2, save, switch it on."
3. Show Try. *"Ipinapakita nito ang bawat hakbang. Walang nababago."* / "It shows each step. Nothing changes."
4. Show a yellow card and the phone. *"Bago magbago ang class record, itatanong muna sa iyo — kahit sa phone."* / "Before your class record changes, it asks you first, even on your phone."
5. *"Tuwing Biyernes, tapos na ang notices bago ka pa umuwi."* / "Every Friday, the notices are ready before you go home."
